ZCode Mobile is the companion app for ZCode, the open-source AI coding agent. Control the agent running on your own computer directly from your phone.

• Read every chat and task — browse sessions running on your desktop or your self-hosted zcode-server, wherever you are.
• Two ways to connect: scan the desktop QR code through the official relay, or connect directly to your own server on your local network — no cloud account needed on the direct path.
• Send instructions, follow streaming output, approve permission requests and keep long-running tasks moving while you are away from your desk.
• A foreground keep-alive service keeps the session reachable, and reconnects pick up right where you left off.

Your data stays yours: the app stores its settings only on your device, ships with no analytics or trackers, and talks only to the servers you choose.
