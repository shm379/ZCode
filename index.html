Privacy Policy

App: ZCode Mobile (Android) · application ID ai.zcode.mobile
Effective date: October 27, 2026 · Version: 1.0

This Privacy Policy describes how the ZCode Mobile Android application (the “App”) handles information. The App is an open-source remote-control client for the ZCode coding agent. It is designed to store your data on your own devices and to connect only to servers you choose. The App does not operate its own backend service and does not collect analytics.

1. Summary

  • The App stores its settings (saved server addresses, access tokens, and a short list of recently opened links) only on your device, in app-private storage.
  • The App has no analytics, no advertising, no trackers, and we never sell or rent data.
  • Chat and task data you see in the App lives on the computer running ZCode (your desktop or your own self-hosted server) — not on any server operated by us.
  • Camera frames used for QR scanning are processed on the device only and are never stored or transmitted.

2. Information the App stores on your device

  • Saved direct-server entries: the address (origin) and, optionally, the access token of self-hosted zcode-server instances you add, so you can reconnect quickly.
  • Recent links: the last five remote-control links you opened (device name, host, and time).
  • Preferences: display and connection preferences needed for the App to work.

All of the above are kept in the App's private storage on your device. Uninstalling the App permanently deletes all of it. The App does not sync this information anywhere.

3. Information processed when you connect

The App is a client. The data you view and send (chat messages, task data, files) is processed by the ZCode instance you connect to, not by us:

  • Official relay connection (zcode.z.ai): if you connect by scanning the QR code shown by the ZCode desktop app, your traffic is relayed by Z.ai's service and your login is handled by Z.ai's sign-in page inside the App's web view. That data is handled under Z.ai's privacy policy; we do not receive a copy.
  • Direct / self-hosted connection: if you add your own zcode-server address, the App talks directly to that server over your network. You choose the operator of that server (in most cases, yourself). Transport is plain HTTP unless you configure HTTPS; the App's security boundary for this path is the server address you saved plus its access token.

4. Permissions

  • Camera — used only to scan QR codes. Frames are analyzed on the device by the on-device ML Kit Barcode Scanning library and are never stored, uploaded, or shared.
  • Internet / network state — required to reach the server you connect to.
  • Notifications & foreground service — an ongoing notification keeps the remote session alive while you use your phone. It carries no message content.

5. Information we share

We do not share information with third parties. There are no advertising networks, analytics providers, or data brokers in the App. The only parties that process your data are the ones you yourself connect to: your relay provider (Z.ai) and the operator of the server you add (usually you).

6. Children

The App is a developer tool, is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Security

Saved tokens are stored in the App's private storage on your device. Connections to the official relay use HTTPS. Connections to self-hosted servers use the scheme you configure (HTTP or HTTPS). No data is transmitted to any server operated by the App's publisher.

8. Your controls

  • Delete saved servers, recent links, and tokens from inside the App (Delete / Clear), or by uninstalling the App.
  • Revoke camera or notification permissions at any time in Android Settings; QR scanning simply stops working without them.
  • Chat/task data deletion is controlled where that data lives: your desktop/self-hosted server, or your Z.ai account for the relay path.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App changes. The effective date above shows the current version; material changes will be reflected in the App's repository release notes.

10. Contact

This App is published as an open-source project. If you have questions about this policy, open an issue at github.com/shm379/ZCode/issues.
